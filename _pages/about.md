I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. [Jun Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=su14mcEAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and Prof. [Xiao Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5FZ6wbAAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). My research focuses on Large Language Models for Recommendation (LLM4Rec), generative recommendation, and generative search.
